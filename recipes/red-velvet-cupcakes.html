About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Notes from this source recipe

  • Temperature equivalents in the source disagree: 330°F (160°C). The Celsius value is retained; Fahrenheit is recalculated.

Dry Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour: 280 g
  • Cornstarch: 19 g (Note: In the video, Aaron mentions using 18 g or 19 g to soften the AP flour into a bakery-soft cake flour texture) (0:59)
  • Dutch-processed cocoa powder: 13 g (2:07)
  • Granulated sugar: 350 g (2:22)
  • Baking soda: 5 g (2:22)
  • Baking powder: 5 g (2:22)
  • Sea salt: 5 mL (2:49) [1]

Wet Ingredients & Liquid Additions

  • Whole milk (for vinegar mix): 120 mL (3:13)
  • White distilled vinegar: 30 g (3:13)
  • Flavorless vegetable oil: 200 mL (3:13)
  • Vanilla paste (or extract): 10 mL (3:30)
  • Large eggs: 4 (3:47)

The Hot Liquid Mixture

  • Whole milk: 240 g (4:19)
  • Unsalted butter: 60 g (4:19)
  • Red food coloring: 1 to 2 packets (Aaron uses gel food coloring available in Austria) (4:40)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Combine the Dry Ingredients

  • In a large mixing bowl, weigh out 280 g of all-purpose flour, 19 g of cornstarch, 13 g of Dutch-processed cocoa powder, 350 g of granulated sugar, 5 g of baking soda, 5 g of baking powder, and 5 mL of sea salt (0:52). Whisk them briefly by hand (0:44). You do not need to sift these ingredients, as the hot liquid added later will dissolve any small lumps. (1:31)

Step 2: Add the First Round of Wet Ingredients

  • Pour 120 mL of whole milk mixed with 30 g of white vinegar directly into the dry mixture (3:13). Add 200 mL of flavorless cooking oil, 10 mL of vanilla paste, and 4 large eggs (3:13). Whisk carefully to combine (3:47). Because this batter is highly liquid, you do not need to worry heavily about over-mixing (3:55).

Step 3: Heat the Milk and Butter Mixture

  • On the stovetop, gently warm 240 g of whole milk along with 60 g of unsalted butter until melted and hot (4:19). This warm liquid addition is an extra step that guarantees the cake crumbs turn out exceptionally tender and moist (5:05).

Step 4: Color the Hot Liquid

  • Squeeze your red food coloring directly into the warm milk and butter mixture before adding it to the cake batter (4:40). Stir it well, adding enough gel until it reaches a deep, vibrant red hue (4:55).

Step 5: Finish the Batter

  • Pour the colored, warm milk mixture straight into your mixing bowl (5:05). Whisk everything together thoroughly by hand until the batter is smooth, thin, and entirely uniform in its bright red color (0:44).

Step 6: Scoop into Jumbo Muffin Pans

  • Preheat your oven to 160 °C (6:26). Line a jumbo-sized muffin or cupcake tin with liners (6:48). Using a large batter scoop, add roughly two scoops of batter to each cavity, being careful not to spill over the sides (7:00).

Step 7: Bake and Cool

  • Slide the muffin tins into the oven and bake at 160 °C for exactly 20 minutes (6:26). Once a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, remove them and allow them to cool fully before frosting (8:03). [1]

For the Cream Cheese Frosting & Topping

  • Cold cream cheese: 440 g (11:23)
  • Butter: 250 g (Slightly chilled, but soft enough to leave a thumbprint) (11:59)
  • Powdered sugar: 400 g (Adjusted down from 600 g for perfect sweetness) (13:21)
  • Vanilla paste: 5 mL (12:50)
  • Walnuts: An extra generous handful (Roughly chopped for topping) (17:12)

Step 8: Frost and Decorate

Once cooled, pipe or spread a generous layer of rich cream cheese frosting over the tops (8:03). Aaron notes that he uses the exact same frosting recipe featured in his Hummingbird/Carrot Cake video. (7:16)
