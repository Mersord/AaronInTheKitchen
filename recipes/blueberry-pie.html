About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

The Blueberry Filling

  • Fresh blueberries: 900 g (0:14)
  • White granulated sugar: 150 g (5:05)
  • Brown sugar: 60 g (5:05)
  • All-purpose flour: 30 g (4:16)
  • Cornstarch: 30 g (Crucial for the pie's structure so it cuts cleanly) (4:36)
  • Fresh lemon juice: 60 mL (approx. 1 medium-to-small lemon) (1:55)
  • Lemon zest: 19.5 mL (the zest of the whole lemon) (0:28)
  • Vanilla paste: 5 mL (2:42)
  • Ground cinnamon: 2 g (2:52)
  • Ground ginger: 1 g (3:03)
  • Ground cardamom: 1 g (Aaron's secret weapon for brightening fruit notes) (3:09)
  • Sea salt: 1 g (3:44)

The Crust & Topping Finish

  • Gluten-free pie crust: 2 pre-made frozen disks (Aaron makes his for a gluten-intolerant guest) (7:34)
  • Unsalted butter: Small knobs for the filling holes + extra melted for brushing the top (17:32)
  • Crystal sugar: A heavy handful for a crunch finish (18:29)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Toss the Fruit Filling

  • Rinse and dump 900 g of fresh blueberries into a large bowl (0:09). Finely zest the yellow skin of a whole lemon directly into the bowl, being careful not to hit the bitter white pith (0:28). Squeeze out exactly 60 mL of the fresh lemon juice and add it to the berries (1:55). Add 5 mL of vanilla paste, 2 g of cinnamon, 1 g of ginger, 1 g of cardamom, and 1 g of sea salt (2:42).

Step 2: Bind the Sugars and Starches

  • Weigh and add 30 g of all-purpose flour, 30 g of cornstarch, 150 g of white sugar, and 60 g of brown sugar directly into the fruit mixture (4:16). Use a large spoon to fold everything together very gently (5:29). Do not smash the berries; you want the oven's high heat to pop them naturally during the bake (6:10). Set the bowl aside (6:32). []

Step 3: Roll the Bottom Crust

  • Generously dust your pastry mat and rolling pin with flour (7:04). If your gluten-free pie dough disks are frozen solid, place them in an 80 °C oven for exactly 1 minute to make them pliable but still cold (8:44). Gently roll the first disk out from the center into a wide circle, patching any small edge cracks with your fingers (9:19).

Step 4: Line and Chill the Pie Tin

  • Roll the flattened dough loosely onto your rolling pin and transfer it over your pie dish (10:34). Press it gently down into the corners, leaving a bit of overhang (10:42). Place the lined tin straight into the freezer to firm up while you prep the top (11:41).

Step 5: Load the Filling and Cut Lattice Strips

  • Preheat your oven to 205 °C (14:57). Pull your chilled bottom crust from the freezer and dump the spiced blueberry filling evenly into it (13:24). Roll out your second dough disk on the floured mat (11:46). Use a pizza cutter to slice long, uniform strips across the dough (14:06). []

Step 6: Weave and Crimp the Edges

  • Lay the cut strips over the top of the blueberries in a woven lattice or a clean zigzag pattern (14:20). Press a small knob of solid butter into the exposed filling spaces between the dough gaps (17:32). Fold the overhanging edge crusts together and press firmly with the tines of a fork to crimp the entire border shut (15:46). []

Step 7: Brush for a Golden Crunch

  • Melt a small amount of extra butter and brush it entirely over the top of the pie lattice strips (18:08). Generously sprinkle coarse crystal sugar over the wet butter to create a sweet, crunchy crust (18:29). Place a baking sheet on the bottom rack of your oven to catch any boiling juices that bubble over the sides (13:52).

Step 8: The Two-Stage Bake and Overnight Cool

Slide the pie into the center rack and bake at 205 °C for the first 20 minutes (16:09). Without removing the pie, drop the oven temperature down to 175 °C and bake for an additional 35 to 45 minutes until the crust is deeply golden and the fruit center is thick and bubbly (16:17). Let it cool for a bare minimum of 4 hours—or overnight if possible—before slicing so the juices set properly (18:59). []
