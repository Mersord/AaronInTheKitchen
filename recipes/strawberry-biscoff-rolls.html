About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

The Dough & Tangzhong

  • Bread flour: 56 g (for Tangzhong) + 504 g (for main dough)
  • Whole milk: 112 g (for Tangzhong) + 240 g (for main dough, warmed to 43 °C
  • Active dry yeast: 7 g
  • Granulated sugar: 100 g
  • Sea salt: 6 g
  • Butter: 250 g (softened)
  • Eggs: 2 large
  • Neutral oil: A splash (for coating the dough and work surface) (6:48)

The Strawberry Jam & Fillings

  • Fresh strawberries: 500 g (for jam) + 300 g (diced, reserved for topping) (8:29)
  • Granulated sugar: 50 g
  • Cornstarch: 30 g (Aaron uses a modified amount to perfectly stabilize the juices) (10:09)
  • Lotus Biscoff cream/spread: 1 jar
  • Biscoff cookies: One 250 g package

Baking & Toppings

  • Heavy cream: 120 g (poured over the rolls before baking)
  • Cream cheese frosting: Leftover or fresh batch (Aaron directs viewers to his carrot cake recipe for this) (26:43)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Make the Tangzhong Starter

  • On the stovetop over high heat, constantly whisk 56 g of bread flour and 112 g of whole milk together (0:31). It will rapidly form a thick paste and pull into a cohesive ball (1:14). Remove it from the heat and set it aside to cool (2:22); this starter locks in moisture so the rolls stay soft for up to 8 days (0:56).

Step 2: Activate Yeast and Combine Wet Ingredients

  • Ensure your remaining 240 g of milk is at 43 °C (2:14). Stir in 7 g of active dry yeast directly into the milk to dissolve (3:22). In your stand mixer bowl, add 504 g of bread flour, 100 g of sugar, and 6 g of sea salt (2:47). Pour in the yeast-milk mixture and add your prepared Tangzhong (0:21).

Step 3: Knead and Incorporate Butter

  • Using a stand mixer on speed 2, begin kneading the dough components (4:11). Once the mixture begins forming a ball around the hook, add all 250 g of softened butter at once (4:58). Crank the mixer up to speed 7 and let it knead for about 8 to 10 minutes until smooth (5:19).

Step 4: The First Rise

  • Lightly coat the inside of a bowl with neutral oil (5:55). Turn your kneaded dough into the bowl, flipping it so the surface is fully protected by the oil (6:48). Cover and let it rest at room temperature for 1 to 4 hours until it has cleanly doubled in size (6:11).

Step 5: Simmer the Chunky Jam

  • While the dough rises, chop 500 g of strawberries and add them to a saucepan with 50 g of sugar and your cornstarch (8:29). Smash the fruits up roughly on the stove (9:07). Simmer until it thickens into a stable jam that won't run out of your rolls during assembly (10:43). Set aside to cool. 

Step 6: Roll Out the Rectangular Dough

  • Punch down your risen dough to release excess gas (13:26). Instead of using flour (which dries out the dough), slick your baking mat and rolling pin with neutral oil (13:53). Roll the dough out carefully into a large, uniform rectangle, making sure not to roll it too thin (14:31).

Step 7: Spread the Fillings and Cut

  • Warm your jar of Biscoff cream in the microwave briefly so it spreads easily (16:31). Slather a thick, even layer of the Biscoff cream entirely across the dough rectangle (17:52). Spread your cooled, chunky strawberry jam right over the top (17:45). Use a pizza cutter to slice the dough into long, even strips (22:01). [1]

Step 8: Assemble and Second Rise

Roll each sliced strip up individually into classic swirled roll shapes and arrange them in your baking pan (21:48). For an extra infusion of moisture, pour 120 g of heavy cream directly over the tops of the arranged rolls (25:10). Allow them to puff up for their final rise.

Step 9: Bake to Golden Perfection

Preheat your oven to 190 °C (23:13). Slide the rolls in and bake for exactly 25 minutes until the tops are golden brown and fluffy (23:27).

For the Cream Cheese Frosting & Topping

  • Cold cream cheese: 440 g (11:23)
  • Butter: 250 g (Slightly chilled, but soft enough to leave a thumbprint) (11:59)
  • Powdered sugar: 400 g (Adjusted down from 600 g for perfect sweetness) (13:21)
  • Vanilla paste: 5 mL (12:50)
  • Walnuts: An extra generous handful (Roughly chopped for topping) (17:12)

Step 10: Dress and Serve

Let the baked rolls cool slightly. Load up your piping bags and drizzle a heavy zigzag pattern of cream cheese frosting and extra melted Biscoff spread over the warm rolls (26:26). Toss your remaining 300 g of fresh diced strawberries and a heavy handful of crushed Biscoff cookie crumbles over the top before diving in (8:38).
