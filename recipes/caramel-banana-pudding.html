About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

Notes from this source recipe

  • The source lists evaporated milk as 680 g, 500 mL, and 2 cups. These are not equivalent. This copy retains the first stated amount, 680 g, by weight; confirm the intended amount before cooking.

The Homemade Pudding Custard

  • Evaporated milk: 680 g (1:17)
  • Brown sugar: 400 g (Used for a richer, deeper flavor than white sugar) (1:08)
  • Large egg yolks: 6 yolks
  • Cornstarch: 30 g (0:15) (Note: The video description mistakenly lists all-purpose flour, but Aaron explicitly measures and uses cornstarch to thicken the scratch-made base) (0:15)
  • Butter: 57 g
  • Vanilla paste: 5 mL

The Light Whipped Cream Fold-In

  • Heavy whipping cream: 237–473 mL
  • Vanilla paste: 5 mL

The Salted Caramel Sauce

  • Granulated sugar: 300 g
  • Butter: 187 g (Ideally a rich European-style butter like Kerrygold to reduce popping) (9:26)
  • Heavy cream: 250 mL (1:31)
  • Sea salt: 5 mL

The Layering Inclusions

  • Bananas: 6 small bananas (18:12)
  • Lotus Biscoff cookies: 1 package (17:56) (An excellent alternative if you live somewhere like Austria where standard Nilla wafers aren't available) (17:48)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prep a Cold Mixing Bowl

  • Place a metal mixing bowl straight into the fridge or freezer (3:49). Whipping cream succeeds best when both the equipment and cream are icy cold (3:57).

Step 2: Start the Pudding Liquid

  • Set a heavy pot over medium heat (0:45). Pour in 400 g of brown sugar, 500 mL of evaporated milk, and 237 mL of heavy cream (0:54). Whisk together and bring the mixture to a boil (5:20).

Step 3: Mix the Egg Base and Temper

  • In a separate bowl, whisk your 6 egg yolks with 30 g of cornstarch until smooth (0:38). Once your milk mixture boils, turn off the heat (5:42). Slowly drizzle a few splashes of the hot liquid into the eggs while whisking vigorously (6:33). This tempers the eggs so you do not accidentally scramble them (6:45).

Step 4: Thicken and Strain the Custard

  • Pour the tempered egg mixture back into the main pot (7:30). Stir as the residual heat cooks down and visibly thickens the pudding (7:42). Remove from the stove and melt in 57 g of butter (8:28). Stir in 5 mL of vanilla paste (12:24). Press the pudding through a fine mesh strainer into a bowl to ensure an ultra-smooth finish (12:11), then set it aside to chill (11:59).

Step 5: Melt the Caramel Sugar

  • In a separate pot over medium heat, add 300 g of regular granulated sugar (2:09). Let it sit undisturbed until the sugar melts down into a golden amber liquid (4:11). Scrape the sides occasionally so it does not burn (6:26).

Step 6: Finish the Salted Caramel Sauce

  • Turn off the heat and drop 187 g of butter into the amber sugar (8:51). Once the butter melts, pour in 237 mL of heavy cream and 5 mL of sea salt (10:31). It will bubble up fiercely but keep stirring until it forms a unified sauce (9:26). Run the caramel through a clean strainer, transfer it to a jar, and let it cool (10:49).

Step 7: Whip the Cream and Fold

Retrieve your frozen metal bowl (14:36). Add your heavy cream and 5 mL of vanilla paste (14:46), then whip until soft-to-medium peaks form (14:57). Pull your chilled pudding base out of the freezer and gently fold the whipped cream into it a little at a time (15:40). This incorporates air, making the pudding beautifully light and fluffy (14:30).

Step 8: Chop the Bananas

Peel your bananas and slice them (16:52). Cutting them on a slight diagonal yields great, chunky bites for the final assembly (18:21).

Step 9: Layer, Chill, and Serve

  • Grab a glass serving dish (20:15). Lay down a foundation layer of Biscoff cookies, followed by a layer of diagonal banana slices (18:33). Drizzle a generous amount of your homemade salted caramel right over the fruit, and then spread a thick layer of the fluffy pudding over the top (19:01). Repeat the layering process until the dish is filled (19:13). Place the completed dish into the freezer or fridge to chill completely before serving (20:11).
