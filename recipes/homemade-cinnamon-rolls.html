About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

Notes from this source recipe

  • Source gives both mass and volume: 16g (approx. 2 tbsps). The metric quantity is retained; no water-density assumption is made.

For the Tangzhong (Dough Starter)

  • Bread flour: 56 g 
  • Whole milk: 112 g 

For the Main Dough

  • Whole milk: 240 g (warmed to 43 °C [01.69, 01.71]
  • Active dry yeast: 7 g
  • Sugar: 100 g 
  • Eggs: 2 large eggs 
  • Bread flour: 504 g (plus an extra 56 g used during the video for a total of 560 g) 
  • Sea salt: 6 g
  • Softened butter: 250 g (room temperature, cut into small pads)

For the Mascarpone Filling

  • Mascarpone cheese: 125 g (slightly softened) 
  • Brown sugar: 220 g 
  • Ground cinnamon: 16 g 
  • Butter: 25 g (added to help soften the cold cheese mix)

For the Baking Step

Heavy cream: 120–150 mL (poured over the buns before baking)

Step-by-Step Baking Instructions

Step 1: Prep the Yeast and Liquids

  • Warm 240 g of whole milk to roughly 43 °C.
  • Pour the warm milk into your mixer bowl and stir in 7 g of active dry yeast . Tip: If your yeast is fresh, you do not need to let it sit and bloom first 

Step 2: Build and Knead the Dough

  • Add 2 large eggs, 560 g of bread flour, 100 g of sugar, and 6 g of sea salt directly into the bowl with the yeast mixture.
  • Fit your stand mixer with the dough hook and turn it on a low speed (between settings 1 and 2).
  • Gradually add your 250 g of softened butter, throwing in one small pad at a time as the machine kneads.
  • Continue kneading for about 20 minutes until the dough gathers into a smooth, glossy brioche-style ball that no longer sticks to the bottom or sides of the bowl 

Step 3: The First Proof

  • Remove the dough ball and transfer it into a clean bowl lightly coated with canola oil.
  • Turn the dough to coat it in the oil, then seal the bowl tightly with plastic wrap.
  • Let the dough rest and rise undisturbed for 30 minutes

Step 4: Mix the Creamy Filling

  • While the dough rises, combine 125 g of mascarpone cheese, 220 g of brown sugar, and 16 g of cinnamon in a bowl.
  • Fixing stiff cheese: If your mascarpone is too cold to mix smoothly, add 25 g of butter and microwave the mixture in brief 15-second bursts until it softens into a spreadable paste

Step 5: Roll, Fill, and Cut

  • Preheat your oven to 190 °C.
  • Dust a pastry mat with a tiny bit of flour and grease your rolling pin with a splash of oil.
  • Turn the dough out onto the mat and roll it into a large rectangle, gently popping any massive air bubbles as you push the rolling pin across.
  • Spread the mascarpone filling smoothly over the surface with a spatula, leaving a narrow clean border around all the edges so it doesn't leak out later.
  • Using a sharp pizza cutter, slice the sheet into even strips about 2.5 cm wide. Roll each individual strip up tightly to shape your buns

Step 6: The Cream Trick & Baking

  • Arrange the shaped rolls side-by-side inside a large baking dish, leaving a little room for them to puff up.
  • Slowly pour 120–150 mL of heavy cream directly over the tops of the rolls right before they go into the oven—this keeps them unbelievably soft and pillowy.
  • Bake at 190 °C for exactly 25 minutes until golden brown. Top them with leftover cream cheese frosting from your Hummingbird Cake if you have any handy 

For the Cream Cheese Frosting & Topping

  • Cold cream cheese: 440 g (11:23)
  • Butter: 250 g (Slightly chilled, but soft enough to leave a thumbprint) (11:59)
  • Powdered sugar: 400 g (Adjusted down from 600 g for perfect sweetness) (13:21)
  • Vanilla paste: 5 mL (12:50)
  • Walnuts: An extra generous handful (Roughly chopped for topping) (17:12)
