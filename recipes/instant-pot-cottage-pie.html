About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

Notes from this source recipe

  • Source gives both mass and volume: 30g (2 tbsps). The metric quantity is retained; no water-density assumption is made.

For the Cheesy Potato Layer

  • Russet potatoes: 500 g (cooked and peeled) 
  • Egg yolks: 2 large egg yolks 
  • Whole milk: 118 mL 
  • Shredded cheese: 500 g total (divided into two 250 g portions) 
  • Potato Seasonings: 19.5 mL sea salt (added in two 10 mL steps) , 29.5 mL black pepper, 15 mL dill, 29.5 mL garlic powder (divided) , 15 mL oregano , 15 mL rosemary, 5 mL lemon pepper , and 10 mL beef bouillon powder. Extra dill for garnishing the top

For the Beef Matrix

  • Ground beef: 1 kg
  • Olive oil: 29.5 mL
  • Large white onion: 1 onion (processed finely)
  • Garlic cloves: 8 to 9 cloves (pressed)
  • Vodka: 100 mL (a heavy splash)
  • Water: 237 mL 
  • All-purpose flour: 30 g (approx. 15 g to thicken the gravy) 
  • Tomato paste: 59–74 mL 
  • Frozen mixed vegetables: 237 mL (peas and carrots work best) 
  • Meat Seasonings: 29.5 mL chicken bouillon powder, 29.5 mL lemon pepper, 29.5 mL Tajín, 5 mL ginger powder, 29.5 mL garlic powder, 10 mL rosemary, 10 mL oregano, 10 mL dill, and 5 mL sea salt

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Mash and Season the Potatoes

  • Preheat your oven to 200 °C.
  • Pass your 500 g of cooked russet potatoes through a potato ricer into a large bowl.
  • Stir in 2 large egg yolks and 118 mL of whole milk.
  • Mix in the first 250 g bag of shredded cheese.
  • Add the potato seasoning base: 19.5 mL salt, 29.5 mL black pepper, 15 mL dill, 29.5 mL garlic powder, 15 mL oregano, 15 mL rosemary, 5 mL lemon pepper, and 10 mL beef bouillon powder. Stir well and set aside 

Step 2: Brown the Meat with Aromatics

  • Set your Instant Pot to its sauté function and add 29.5 mL of olive oil.
  • Drop in 1 kg of ground beef (it is perfectly fine if it is still halfway frozen). Begin breaking it apart.
  • Pulse your onion in a food processor and press 9 cloves of garlic. Toss them directly into the pot with the browning beef.
  • Pour in a 100 mL splash of vodka to help build depth of flavor

Step 3: Spice and Thicken the Gravy

  • While the beef continues cooking down, add the meat seasonings: 29.5 mL chicken bouillon, 29.5 mL lemon pepper, 29.5 mL Tajín, 5 mL ginger powder, 29.5 mL garlic powder, 10 mL rosemary, 10 mL oregano, 10 mL dill, and 5 mL salt.
  • Pour in 237 mL of water and stir to dissolve the bouillon powder.
  • Add 59 mL of tomato paste to change the liquid baseline.
  • Dust 30 g of all-purpose flour over the mixture. Stir it consistently for a few minutes until the sauce reduces into a thick, chunky chili-like consistency. Note: It is fine if some meat pieces remain slightly pink before baking

Step 4: Incorporate Vegetables and Assemble

  • Turn off the Instant Pot heat. Stir in 237 mL of frozen mixed vegetables and let them sit in the hot meat mixture for 2 minutes to thaw.
  • Transfer the entire thick beef mix into the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish.
  • Sprinkle a light coating of cheese from your second bag directly over the hot meat layer

Step 5: Top and Bake

  • Spread your seasoned mashed potatoes evenly across the top of the cheese and beef layer.
  • Empty the remaining shredded cheese from the second bag across the entire top surface of the potatoes to ensure a gorgeous crust forms.
  • Bake uncovered at 200 °C for exactly 20 minutes until the top cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  • Garnish with an extra pinch of dill right out of the oven and serve hot
