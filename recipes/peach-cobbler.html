About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

Peach Filling

  • Fresh peaches (left unpeeled to prevent slipping while cutting) (0:08)
  • 100 g white sugar (3:12)
  • 50 g brown sugar (3:26)
  • 14 g cornstarch (3:26)
  • 100 mL water (3:26)
  • A splash of white vinegar (used as a substitute for lemon juice) (3:56)
  • 29.5 mL ground ginger (4:17)
  • 29.5 mL ground cinnamon (4:24)
  • 19.5 mL salt (4:36)

Crust & Assembly

  • 180 g all-purpose flour (5:19)
  • 14 g baking soda (5:46)
  • 4 g salt (5:34)
  • 29.5 mL ground ginger (6:08)
  • 29.5 mL ground cinnamon (6:08)
  • 100 mL water (plus a teeny splash more if needed) (5:19)
  • 250 mL heavy whipping cream (6:56)
  • 150 mL melted butter (7:50)

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Prep and Cook the Peaches

  1. Slice and core your fresh peaches, leaving the skin on for easier handling (1:01).
  2. In a small bowl, mix together the white sugar, brown sugar, cornstarch, and 100 mL of water (3:00).
  3. Pour this dry and wet sugar mixture over the peaches in a pot (3:46).
  4. Add a splash of white vinegar, ginger, cinnamon, and 19.5 mL of salt (3:56).
  5. Cook on the stovetop over low heat until the peaches brown slightly and a thick syrup develops (4:49). Remove from heat and set aside (6:40).

Step 2: Make the Batter

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, 4 g of salt, and 29.5 mL each of ginger and cinnamon to ensure the dough is flavorful (5:19).
  2. Pour in 100 mL of water and the heavy whipping cream (6:56).
  3. Whisk everything together until it reaches a thick pancake batter consistency (add a tiny splash of water if it is too thick) (8:17).

Step 3: Assemble the Cobbler

  1. Pour 150 mL of melted butter into the bottom of your baking dish (7:50).
  2. Pour the thick batter directly over the butter and spread it out evenly across the dish (8:17). Do not mix the batter into the butter.
  3. Carefully spoon the cooked peaches and their syrup right on top of the batter, spacing the fruit out evenly (8:55). The crust will naturally rise up around the fruit as it bakes (9:07).

Step 4: Bake

  1. Bake in a preheated oven at 190 °C for 50 minutes (9:26).
  2. Serve warm, ideally topped with vanilla ice cream (9:58)!
