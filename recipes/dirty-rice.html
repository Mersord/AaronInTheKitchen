About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

The Inclusions & Base

  • Cheese sausage: 1 package (Aaron specifically uses an Austrian Käsekrainer cheese sausage) (3:17)
  • Onion: 1 large (chopped) (0:05)
  • Garlic: 1 whole head (pressed) (1:59)
  • Olive oil: 29.5 mL (1:00)
  • All-purpose flour: 5–7.5 g (Note: The video description says 15 g, but Aaron specifies 5–7.5 g to bind the liquids) (5:31)
  • White rice: 600 g (6:20)
  • Chicken stock: 946 mL (6:43)
  • Cold water: A small splash (Optional, to slow down hot garlic) (2:39)

Seasonings & Spices

  • Garlic powder: 29.5 mL total (3:54)
  • Onion powder: 29.5 mL total (4:31)
  • Creole seasoning (Tony Chachere's): 29.5 mL total (4:05)
  • Slap Ya Mama Cajun seasoning: 29.5 mL total (4:05)
  • Steak seasoning (or all-purpose seasoning): 29.5 mL total (4:43)
  • White pepper: 10 mL total (3:54)
  • Sea salt: 10 mL total (4:26)
  • Dill: 10 mL total (3:46)
  • Rosemary: 10 mL total (4:14) 

(Note: Aaron originally skips the celery and beef bouillon mentioned in his standard description because it's a holiday weekend, but notes you can absolutely add them back into yours!) (0:37)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Sauté the Aromatics

  • Set your Instant Pot to its sauté function and add 29.5 mL of olive oil (0:54). Drop in your chopped onion and press an entire head of garlic straight into the pot using a garlic press (0:05).
    Pro-Tip: If your garlic begins to cook too fast or look like it might burn while you multitask, toss in a tiny splash of cold water to drop the pot's temperature (2:26).

Step 2: Brown the Sausage

  • Slice your cheese sausage and drop it into the sizzling pot (3:24). Sauté the mixture for a few minutes just until the sausage takes on a nice bit of color (3:33). As it heats up, the pockets of cheese inside the sausage will slowly melt out into the oil, creating a rich baseline flavor (5:16).

Step 3: Shake in the First Round of Spices

  • Measure out and add 15 mL each of white pepper, garlic powder, Tony Chachere's Creole seasoning, Slap Ya Mama, onion powder, and steak seasoning (3:54). Toss in 5 mL each of dill, rosemary, and fine sea salt (3:46). Stir well to coat the meat and onions entirely.

Step 4: Add the Binder

  • Sprinkle 5–7.5 g of all-purpose flour directly over the meat and spices, mixing well (5:31). This creates a light thickening layer that helps the rendered oils, melting cheese, and spices stick flush to your rice grains later (5:16).

Step 5: Incorporate Rice and Stock

  • Pour 600 g of white rice into the pot (6:20). Stir vigorously for a moment so the individual grains get fully slicked in the spiced oil (6:28). Pour in 946 mL of chicken stock (6:43). Take a sturdy spoon and scrape the entire bottom surface of the inner liner carefully to lift up any stuck flour or spices so the machine won't trigger a "burn" warning (7:04).

Step 6: Double Down on Flavor

  • Give the liquid a quick taste test (7:29). To truly make the dish stand out, add 15 mL of all your core seasonings directly into the liquid before sealing (7:43).

Step 7: Pressure Cook

  • Allow the liquid inside the pot to reach a rolling boil (7:56). Once boiling, lock the Instant Pot lid in place, set it to manual pressure cook on high for 10 minutes, and let it do its magic (8:22).

Step 8: Fluff and Serve

  • Once the timer goes off, let the pressure release, open the lid, and fluff the dirty rice (8:22). The final texture will be wonderfully rich, juicy, and packed with a robust but balanced savory punch (9:26).
