About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

The Cake Batter

  • All-purpose flour: 300 g (0:42)
  • Cornstarch: 18 g (Aaron's secret to mimicking soft cake flour) (6:11)
  • Vegetable oil: 350 mL (2:44)
  • Brown sugar: 220 g (3:12)
  • Granulated sugar: 200 g (3:19)
  • Large eggs: 4 (3:19)
  • Grated carrots: 313 g (4:49)
  • Vanilla paste: 29.5 mL (4:23)
  • Baking powder: 7 g (1:14)
  • Baking soda: 5 g (1:14)
  • Fine sea salt: 3 g (2:06)
  • Cinnamon: 5 mL (2:18)
  • Ginger: 5 mL (2:18)
  • Ground nutmeg: 10 mL (8:15)
  • Ground cardamom: 7.5 mL (8:33)
  • Golden raisins: 100 g (Added by special request) (0:15)
  • Mixed nuts: 100 g total (A 50/50 blend of chopped hazelnuts and walnuts) (4:49)

For the Cream Cheese Frosting & Topping

  • Cold cream cheese: 440 g (11:23)
  • Butter: 250 g (Slightly chilled, but soft enough to leave a thumbprint) (11:59)
  • Powdered sugar: 400 g (Adjusted down from 600 g for perfect sweetness) (13:21)
  • Vanilla paste: 5 mL (12:50)
  • Walnuts: An extra generous handful (Roughly chopped for topping) (17:12)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prep the Oven and Baking Pan

  • Preheat your oven to 175 °C (10:31). Take a standard 22.9 × 33 cm baking dish and line the entire bottom and sides completely with parchment paper (4:17). Use kitchen shears to snip the corners so the paper folds and lays perfectly flat (9:54).

Step 2: Mix the Dry Ingredients

  • Set up your kitchen scale (0:50). Weigh out and whisk together 300 g of all-purpose flour, 18 g of cornstarch, 7 g of baking powder, 5 g of baking soda, and 3 g of fine sea salt (0:42). Whisk in your dry spices: cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and cardamom (2:18).

Step 3: Combine the Wet Elements & Inclusions

  • Pour 350 mL of vegetable oil directly into the bowl (2:36). Add the brown sugar, granulated sugar, four eggs, and 29.5 mL of vanilla paste (3:12). Add your 313 g of freshly grated carrots, the 100 g nut blend, and the golden raisins (4:49).

Step 4: Mix the Batter Carefully by Hand

  • Mix everything together gently using a hand spoon or spatula (5:14). Scrape the absolute bottom of the bowl and drag it up to ensure no dry pockets remain (7:19). Do not use an electric mixer here; carrot cake batter is incredibly easy to over-mix! (5:14)

Step 5: Bake and Cool

  • Pour your smooth, rich batter into your fully lined 9x13 pan (10:23). Bake at 175 °C for exactly 30 minutes (10:31). Let the cake cool completely before attempting to frost it.

Step 6: Whip up the Frosting

  • While the cake cools, place 440 g of cold cream cheese and 250 g of slightly chilled butter into a clean bowl (11:23). Mix on a low speed with a hand mixer to combine them (12:19). Blend in 5 mL of vanilla paste and 400 g of powdered sugar until the texture is completely smooth (12:50).

Step 7: Frost and Garnish

  • Pour your cream cheese frosting smoothly over the top of the sheet cake (15:01). Use your spatula to create texturized, elegant "sideways swoops" in the frosting while ensuring no cake peeks through (16:25). Roughly chop a handful of walnuts into large, chunky pieces and sprinkle them generously over the top (17:20).
