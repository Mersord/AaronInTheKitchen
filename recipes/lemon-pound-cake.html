About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

For the Cake Batter

  • All-purpose flour: 240 g
  • Baking powder: 7 g
  • Sea salt: 5 mL
  • Butter: 250 g (cold) (5:48)
  • Granulated white sugar: 100 g
  • Brown sugar: 150 g
  • Fresh lemons: 2 lemons (to get 44.5 mL of zest) (0:22)
  • Whole milk: 120 mL
  • Eggs: 4 large eggs
  • Dark maple syrup: 80 mL (the secret twist!) (8:30)

For the Quick Lemon Glaze:

  • Powdered sugar: 120 g (10:18)
  • Whole milk: 10 mL (10:31)
  • Fresh lemon juice: 15 mL (10:31)

Step-by-Step Baking Instructions

Step 1: Prep and Zesting

  • Preheat your oven to 175 °C (8:08).
  • Zest the outer yellow layer of 2 fresh lemons until you have 44.5 mL of zest (0:22).
  • Tip: Use a microplane upside down to collect the zest on top (1:37), and avoid grating into the bitter white pith (0:33).

Step 2: Make the Lemon Sugar

  • Weigh 100 g of white granulated sugar and 150 g of brown sugar into a bowl (3:32).
  • Add the lemon zest directly into the sugar mixture (2:02).
  • Rub the zest and sugars thoroughly between your hands (or pulse in a food processor) until the texture resembles wet sand (2:11). This releases the essential oils from the zest for an explosive flavor (2:16).

Step 3: Reverse Creaming & Batter MixAdd

  • Add 250 g of cold butter into the lemon sugar and cream them together (5:32).
  • Add 240 g of all-purpose flour, 7 g of baking powder, and 5 mL of sea salt (6:43).
  • Pour in 120 mL of whole milk, crack in 4 large eggs, and add the special 80 mL twist of dark maple syrup (7:20).
  • Mix everything until just combined, ensuring you scrape any remaining flour from the edges of the bowl (9:09).

Step 4: Fill and Bake

  • Generously spray a bundt pan (a silicone bundt mold helps prevent cracking) or a standard loaf pan (8:46).
  • Pour the batter in and tap the pan against the counter to distribute it evenly (9:14).
  • Bake at 175 °C for exactly 1 hour (8:08). Let it cool down after baking.

Step 5: Glaze and Assemble

  • In a small bowl, combine 120 g of powdered sugar, 10 mL of whole milk, and 15 mL of fresh lemon juice (10:18).
  • Whisk with a fork or mini whisk until it forms a thick, smooth glaze (11:08).
  • Drizzle the glaze over the top of the cooled pound cake, letting it run beautifully down the sides.
