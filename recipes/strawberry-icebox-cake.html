About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Ingredient conversion reference · Conversion notes

Notes from this source recipe

  • The source lists heavy cream as 1 cup (200 mL). This copy uses the explicit metric amount, 200 mL, and calculates the US view from that amount.
  • The source lists cream cheese as 265 g (about 8 oz / 1 block). This copy retains 265 g, not an assumed block size.
  • Source equivalents disagree: 1 cup (200 ml). The explicit metric quantity is retained.

Cream Filling

  • Cream Cheese: 265 g
  • Heavy Whipping Cream: 200 mL
  • Powdered Sugar: 120 g
  • Lemon Zest: Zest of 1 whole lemon

Layers & Assembly

  • Fresh Strawberries: 250 g (washed, topped, and sliced)
  • Biscoff Cookies: 1 pack (from a ~200 g package) 

Equipment Needed

  • Loaf pan / cake dish lined with plastic wrap
  • Mixing bowl & whisk
  • Kitchen scale

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Prep the Dish & Ingredients

  • Line a loaf pan/cake dish with plastic wrap, leaving enough overhang to pull the cake out later.
  • Slice the washed fresh strawberries into thin, flat slices.
  • Tip: If the cream cheese is too cold, submerge the sealed container in a bowl of hot water for a few minutes to quickly soften it.

Step 2: Make the Cream Filling:

  • In a large bowl, combine the heavy whipping cream (200 mL) and softened cream cheese (265 g).
  • Add the zest of 1 whole lemon.
  • Whisk continuously by hand until thick and smooth.
  • Add the powdered sugar (120 g) and whisk until fully incorporated.

Step 3: Assemble the Cake

  • Base Layer: Spread a layer of the cream filling evenly over the bottom of the plastic-wrap-lined pan.
  • Cookie Layer: Place a single layer of Biscoff cookies on top of the cream layer.
  • Fruit Layer: Arrange a layer of sliced strawberries over the cookies.
  • Top Layer: Pour the remaining cream filling over the top, spreading it evenly to cover the fruit and cookies completely.

Step 4: Chill & Serve

  • Fold the overhanging plastic wrap over the top to seal the cake.
  • Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or ideally overnight, so the cookies absorb moisture from the cream and soften into a cake-like texture.
  • Unmold using the plastic wrap overhang, slice, and serve.
