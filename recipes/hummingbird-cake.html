About these measurements

Metric is the default. US cups are 236.588 mL, not 250 mL. Flour, sugar and other supported dry ingredients use ingredient-specific weight conversions; they are not treated as water. Displayed conversions are rounded.

Where a reliable weight-to-volume conversion is unavailable, weighed ingredients use ounces or pounds in the US view; spoon-measured ingredients use millilitres in the metric view. Ounces (oz) mean weight, not fluid ounces. Counts, pinches, and “to taste” remain unchanged.

For baking and very small quantities, a kitchen scale is more reliable than estimated cups. Changing units does not adjust the recipe for a fan oven or a different pan.

Notes from this source recipe

  • Source equivalents disagree: 1 tsp (approx. 5g). The explicit metric quantity is retained.
  • Source equivalents disagree: 1/2 tsp (approx. 3g). The explicit metric quantity is retained.
  • Source gives both mass and volume: 1 1/2 cups (approx. 350g). The metric quantity is retained; no water-density assumption is made.

For the Cake Base

  • All-purpose flour: 360 g
  • Baking soda: 5 g (2:37)
  • Baking powder: 3 g (2:51)
  • Sea salt: 5 mL (2:51)
  • Ground cinnamon: 5–10 mL (3:22)
  • Ginger powder: 5 mL (3:22)
  • Granulated white sugar: 200 g (4:13)
  • Brown sugar: 200–210 g (4:13)
  • Vegetable oil: 350 g (4:48)
  • Eggs: 3 large eggs (4:57)
  • Vanilla paste: 10 mL (or vanilla extract) (5:22)
  • Ripe bananas: 5 bananas (0:49)
  • Crushed pineapple: 227 g (with the juice included) (1:23)
  • Walnuts: 120 g (chopped) (5:22)

For the Cream Cheese Frosting

  • Butter: 340 g, ice-cold (9:16)
  • Philadelphia cream cheese: 250 g+, ice-cold (9:35)
  • Vanilla: 5 mL (10:32)
  • Powdered sugar: Approx. 723 g (12:52)

Step-by-Step Baking Instructions

Step 1: Prep and Oven Setup

  • Preheat your oven to 175 °C (3:52).
  • Grease three 20.3 cm silicone cake molds with non-stick spray (6:46).

Step 2: Mash the Wet Baseos

  • Peel 5 very ripe bananas and place them in a mixing bowl (0:49).
  • Tip: If your bananas aren't ripe enough, bake them at 175 °C for 10 minutes until black and soft (0:37).
  • Mash them completely using a potato ricer or fork (0:49).
  • Add 227 g of crushed pineapples with their juice and mix with the ricer until combined (1:23).

Step 3: Combine Dry Spices & Sugars

  • Place the bowl on a kitchen scale and zero it out (4:13).
  • Weigh in 360 g of all-purpose flour, 5 g of baking soda, and 3 g of baking powder (2:21).
  • Add 5 mL of sea salt, 5–10 mL of cinnamon, and 5 mL of ginger powder (2:51).
  • Weigh out 200 g of white granulated sugar and 200–210 g of brown sugar directly into the mix (4:13).

Step 4: Wet Ingredients & Walnuts

  • Pour in 350 g of vegetable oil, 3 large eggs, and 10 mL of vanilla paste (4:48).
  • Toss in 115–120 g of chopped walnuts (5:22).
  • Scale safety rule: Remove the bowl from your kitchen scale before mixing so you do not damage the scale's calibration (5:32). [1]

Step 5: Mix and Bake

  • Using a simple spatula, fold the batter completely by hand (1:14).
  • Mix only until the ingredients are just combined and no flour lumps remain (1:04). Do not overmix.
  • Divide the batter evenly among the three prepared pans using a scale for accuracy (approx. 464–500 g per pan) (7:33).
  • Bake at 175 °C for roughly 25 minutes. Let the cakes cool entirely overnight before frosting (8:36).

🍦 Step-by-Step Frosting & Assembly

Step 1: Beat the Base

  • To keep the frosting stable, use ice-cold butter and cream cheese straight from the fridge (8:44).
  • Add 340 g of cold butter pieces and 250 g of cold cream cheese into a bowl with 5 mL of vanilla (9:16).
  • Use a hand mixer to beat them together (10:52). Because it is cold, it will take a moment to integrate (11:33).
  • Fixing lumps: If the butter chunks are stubborn, transfer the mix to a microwave-safe bowl and nuke it for 15 seconds to soften slightly (14:40).

Step 2: Add Sugar

  • Gradually mix in up to 723 g of powdered sugar (12:52).
  • Beat on high speed until the texture turns thick, smooth, and glossy (13:34).

Step 3: Assemble and Decorate

  • Place your first cake layer down on a piece of parchment paper on a cake turntable (18:03).
  • Spread a generous layer of cream cheese frosting evenly over the layer using a cake spatula (18:17).
  • Stack the second layer, add more frosting, and top with the third cake layer (18:46).
  • Coat the outside of the cake. Use rustic, decorative "swoops" with your spatula on the top and sides for a clean, homemade look (22:05).
  • Chill the cake in the refrigerator before serving (12:23).
